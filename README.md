##Business problem:
#A city mobility team wants to understand taxi demand, revenue, fare behavior, trip duration, tipping, and operational anomalies.

###Main questions:

1. Which hours and days have the most demand?
2. Which pickup zones generate the most trips and revenue?
3. How do fare, distance, and duration relate?
4. Which trips look suspicious or invalid?
5. How does tipping vary by payment type, zone, and time?
6. Can we build a simple baseline model to predict fare or duration?
